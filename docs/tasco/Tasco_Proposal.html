Proposal to Tasco: AI-assisted compulsory motor insurance that customers are glad to hold

GrowERP Insurance · BeyondPilot “AI for Insurance × Tasco” · submitted by GrowERP (Hans Bakker) · October 2026 · working prototype: tasco.growerp.net

1. Business problem

Compulsory motor insurance (TNDS) is regulated into sameness: identical cover and price from every insurer, bought only to comply. So nobody competes on experience, and it shows along the whole journey:

  • Buy: cover written as legal text; renewals forgotten; nothing fitted to the individual driver.
  • Carry: a paper certificate that gets lost; no instant, verifiable proof at a roadside check.
  • Claim: calls and visits to report, photos and documents chased afterwards, slow assessment, no view of status. Every question goes to a hotline.

Tasco sees the whole journey (the car, VETC on the road, the service centre, the claim) and can turn this compliance product into a reason customers stay in its ecosystem, without touching price.

2. Proposed AI solution

One app for the driver (web/PWA, Android, iOS; Vietnamese and English) on the same system the agency or insurer runs its policies, claims and accounting on.

  • Digital policy card with live QR proof. Police or a garage scan it with any phone and see valid or not, plate masked, no personal data.
  • Plain-language cover. An LLM explains each policy from its own limits and deductibles, in the customer's language, inventing nothing.
  • Guided claim (first notice of loss) in minutes. Incident type, time and place, and a photo checklist (plate, vehicle, damage, other party), with explicit consent.
  • AI photo triage for the adjuster. A vision model reports damaged parts, severity, whether the car is drivable, whether the plate matches the policy, inconsistencies and missing evidence, plus a suggested next step (garage, roadside, inspection). Advice only: the AI never approves, denies or prices a claim.
  • Transparent status. A timeline with a plain explanation of every step, and an email (later Zalo/SMS) on each change.
  • 24/7 assistant. Runs as the customer, so it sees only their own policies and claims plus the insurer's knowledge base. It hands off to a person when needed.
  • Calm renewal. Reminders at 30, 7 and 1 day, and a one-tap renewal request to staff. No discounts, cashback or artificial urgency anywhere.

All of this already runs in the hosted prototype. The model is set per tenant (Gemini, Anthropic or OpenAI); the source is open (Apache 2.0).

3. PoC scope

In scope

  • One segment: TNDS policyholders of one Tasco Auto dealer or branch (proposed 300–1,000 policies).
  • Card and QR proof, plain-language cover, guided claim with AI triage, status timeline, assistant, and renewal reminders.
  • Staff workspace for agents and adjusters, in Tasco branding and Vietnamese.

Out of scope for the PoC

  • Automated claim decisions or payments; pricing and underwriting changes.
  • Full core-system integration: CSV import and manual status updates stand in (section 7).
  • Telematics or usage-based data.

4. Expected business value

  • Lower claims handling cost: complete first notice with photos, and a first read for the adjuster instead of phone calls.
  • Fewer service contacts: status and questions answered in the app, and escalations routed with context.
  • Better retention: timely, calm renewal and a product customers actually use, opening doors to Tasco's garage, roadside and vehicle-care services.
  • Compliance and trust: verifiable proof on the road, consent and audit built in.

9. Path to production

  • Roll out: from one dealer to all Tasco Auto dealers, then voluntary motor, and then other lines on the same platform. The data model already covers any policy type.
  • Integrate and operate: REST connections to the core policy and claims systems, VETC, the garage and roadside network, and Zalo, or embed our screens in Tasco's customer app. Multi-tenant, containerised and deployable in Vietnam; the AI queue absorbs peaks (Tết renewals, rainy-season claims) without slowing claim intake.
  • Govern: human-in-the-loop decisions, audit logs of status changes and every AI call, monthly AI budgets per tenant, and periodic review of triage quality with adjusters.
