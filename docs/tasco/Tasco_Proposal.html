Proposal to Tasco: an AI renewal engine for compulsory motor insurance (TNDS)

GrowERP Insurance · BeyondPilot “AI for Insurance × Tasco” · submitted by GrowERP (Hans Bakker) · October 2026 · working system: tasco.growerp.net

1. Business problem

TNDS is the same cover at the same regulated price from every insurer, so customers renew late, with whoever reminds them at the right moment, or not at all. Discounts are not allowed and no answer. Renewal is won on timing, clarity and an easy next step, and a person when the customer wants one. Today reminders go out broadly, on fixed schedules and channels. Telesales calls without knowing who is likely to renew. Every extra step between “your cover ends” and “renewed” loses customers.

2. Proposed AI solution: one renewal journey across VETC, Zalo and telesales

Briefing trackWhat runs today in the demo system (synthetic data)
B. Lead scoring and enrichmentEvery customer due within 60 days gets an explainable score (renewal history, reactions to earlier reminders, consent, profile completeness, timing) with plain reasons, a band, profile completeness and missing fields, and the next best channel and time of day, taken from the consented channel the customer reacts to most.
C. Renewal engineAI drafts each reminder in Vietnamese from the policy facts only: calm, no discounts, one next step, and how to stop. It is sent on the chosen channel (Zalo / VETC push / SMS / email; Zalo and VETC shown as phone previews until connected). One tap opens a private renewal page with no app and no login: the customer confirms the car with 3 plate characters, then renews, asks for a call, or says “sold / stop”.
A. AI voice botA Vietnamese AI call (speech by Gemini): it asks permission, confirms the vehicle, answers questions from the policy only and hands over to telesales with a summary, transcript and outcome code. Opt-out works at every step; the AI never quotes prices or decides.
D. Value beyond discountThe renewal page shows service instead of price: the digital certificate with a live QR check, guided claims with photo triage and status tracking, and roadside and service-centre help. Specific offers are marked “subject to compliance review”.
Telesales and measurementA telesales queue (who asked for a call, AI hand-overs, renewal requests) with CRM-style outcome codes; “Renewed” renews the policy. A funnel by stage, channel and score band: reminded → opened → visited → confirmed → requested → renewed.

3. PoC scope (4 weeks, proportionate)

In: one segment of TNDS policies due for renewal (for example one Tasco Auto dealer, or customers of one VETC region), scored and reminded through the renewal page; telesales hand-over; AI call for consenting customers; the funnel. Start on masked data, real data after compliance approval. Out: instant policy issuance (staff issue as today), new insurer APIs, pricing, and any customer contact without consent.

5. Fit: workflows, data, consent and integration

  • Data fields needed: policy number, insurer, start and end date, plate, vehicle; customer name, phone, email; consent per channel with date and source; past renewal and reminder events; call outcomes. A synthetic book is used today; masked data first.
  • Consent and privacy (Decree 13/2023): contact only on channels with recorded consent; “stop” honoured everywhere; the renewal link is private (random token, plate check before details). Personal data stays on Tasco-approved hosting in Vietnam if required.
  • Integration (to validate, not assumed): Zalo OA / VETC push sending and deep links, SMS gateway, a telephony line for the AI call, CRM call-outcome codes, and the issuance hand-off. Each has a manual fallback for the PoC (preview pages, staff dial-out, CSV import/export), so nothing blocks week 2.
  • People decide: staff issue renewals and own every call outcome; the AI drafts, scores, explains and hands over.

7. Cost: open source, pay only for what runs

GrowERP is free and open source (Apache 2.0): no licence fees, no lock-in, Tasco keeps the code. Tasco pays only for the production environment, enhancements and support.
  • Production environment (managed by us, or on Tasco's cloud): priced by the number of users, quoted once the pilot scope is known; the PoC runs on our infrastructure at no charge.
  • Enhancements (Zalo OA, VETC, telephony, CRM and issuance integrations): a fixed price per enhancement, set by its size once scoped in week 1.
  • Support and operations: priced by the number of users, together with the environment.
  • AI usage passed through at cost (Gemini today, switchable per tenant): drafting a reminder costs a fraction of a cent; an AI call is dominated by speech time. Every call is logged per tenant under a monthly budget.

8. From PoC to production

  • Scale: from one segment to all TNDS renewals, then voluntary motor (where lead scoring and service value matter more), on the same multi-tenant platform.
  • Integrate: live Zalo OA and VETC notifications, telephony for the AI call, the CRM, and policy issuance; claims and digital certificate (already built) as the next phase.
  • Govern: audit trail of every contact, consent registry, explainable scores reviewed with telesales, monthly AI budget per tenant.
