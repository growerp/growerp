## Scenario A: The very first account on a new GrowERP system

*For the person who installs GrowERP. This account becomes the GrowERP system administrator.*

### Registration

1. Open the Admin app. The **start screen** shows the GrowERP logo and two buttons: **Login** and **Register new company and admin**.
2. Tap **Register new company and admin**.
3. The **Registration** screen opens. Enter your first name, last name and email address. The note *"A temporary password will be sent by email"* is shown. Tap **Register**.
4. The message *"Registration successful. You can now login with the password sent by email"* appears, and you return to the start screen.
5. Check your email for your password.

### First login

1. Tap **Login**. On the **Login with Existing user name** screen, enter your email and the password from the email, then tap **Login**.
2. The **Company Setup** screen appears:
   - **Enter company name**: already filled in as *GrowERP*.
   - **Currency** and **Accounting year starts**.
   - There is no demo-data option: the system company always starts empty.
3. Tap **Complete Setup**. Setting up takes a moment. The public GrowERP website, the ERP questionnaire and the marketing content are installed at the same time.
4. You arrive on the **dashboard**. No welcome message and no questionnaire are shown, and there is no trial period or payment for the system company.

### Second and later logins

Log in and go straight to the dashboard. No extra screens ever appear.

## Scenario B: Starting a new company (free trial)

*For anyone who registers a new business.*

### Registration

1. Open the app (for example at growerp.com). The **start screen** shows **Login** and **Register new company and admin**.
   - *On the website:* if you already typed your name, email and password on the web start page, the app registers you automatically. If you typed only your email, the Registration screen opens with your email filled in.
2. On the **Registration** screen, enter your first name, last name and email, then tap **Register**.
3. The message *"Registration successful…"* appears. Your password arrives by email, unless you chose one on the web start page.

### First login

1. Tap **Login** and enter your email and password.
2. The **Company Setup** screen appears:
   - **Enter company name**: the name of your business.
   - **Currency** and **Accounting year starts**.
   - **Load Demo Data**: tick this to try GrowERP with example products, customers and orders.
3. Tap **Complete Setup**. Your **14-day free trial** starts now.
4. *If you ticked demo data:* the **Setting up your company** screen appears with *"Your demo data is being created. This takes a minute or so, you can leave this open."* You continue automatically when it is done.
   - If it takes longer than expected, you can keep waiting or tap **Continue**. The data keeps appearing as it is created.
   - If the demo data fails, you are told so and can continue without it. Your company is set up.
5. You arrive on the **dashboard**. On top of it you see two screens, one after the other:
   - **Welcome to GrowERP!**: your company, your trial end date, the features included, and *"No credit card required during trial."* Tap **Get Started**.
   - **Set up GrowERP for your business**: a short questionnaire. Your answers tailor the menu, for example by hiding Accounting if you don't need it. Tap **Skip** if you don't want to answer.
6. You are now in the app.

### Second and later logins

Log in and go straight to the dashboard. The welcome and the questionnaire are **shown only once**, in the first app you use.

## Scenario C: Joining a company that already uses GrowERP

*For employees or customers invited to a company's app, usually through a company-specific link or app.*

### Registration

1. The **start screen** shows the company's logo and name, with **Login** and **Register as new user of *&lt;Company&gt;***.
2. Tap it, fill in the **Registration** screen (first name, last name, email), and tap **Register**.
3. The message *"Registration successful…"* appears, and your password arrives by email.

### First login

1. Tap **Login** and enter your email and password.
2. There is no company setup, because the company already exists.
3. You arrive on the **dashboard**. The **Set up GrowERP for your business** questionnaire is shown once, and you can **Skip** it.
4. The company administrator decides what you can see and do.

### Second and later logins

Straight to the dashboard.

> If you see *"Tenant setup not complete. Please contact administrator."*, the company's administrator has not finished their own first login yet (Scenario B, step 2).

## Scenario D: Logging in after the trial has ended

*For any user of a company whose trial or subscription has expired.*

1. After you log in, the **Subscription** screen appears with *"Your subscription has expired. Please renew to continue."*
2. Choose a **Payment plan**, enter your credit card details, and tap **Pay now**.
3. Once payment succeeds you continue to the dashboard as usual.

The system company from Scenario A never sees this screen.

---

## Scenario E: Forgot password

1. On the **Login** screen, tap **Forgot password?**. On the **Send new password** screen, enter your email to receive a temporary password.
2. Log in with the temporary password. The **Create password** screen appears.
3. Enter your new password twice. It needs at least 8 characters, including a letter, a digit and a special character (`!@#$%^&+=`). Then tap **Submit new password**.
4. You continue to the dashboard.

## App differences

All GrowERP apps use the same start, registration and login screens. What differs:

| App | What is different for you |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Exactly as described above. This is the main public entry point for new companies. |
| **Hotel** | Demo data contains hotel rooms and reservations. Setup prepares the room types. |
| **Rental** | Demo data contains rental equipment and bookings. |
| **Freelance** | As described above. |
| **Marketing** | Company Setup does **not** ask for currency or accounting year (US dollars and January are used). Demo data has no orders. |
| **Agents** | Company Setup does **not** ask for currency or accounting year (US dollars and January are used). |
| **Academy** | Usually opened through a school's or company's link, so it follows **Scenario C** (join as a learner). |
| **Support** | For GrowERP support staff only. There is **no Register button**, and only system-support accounts can log in. No setup, trial, welcome or questionnaire screens appear. |

**Using more than one app:** your account works in every GrowERP app of your company. The welcome and questionnaire appear only in the first app you use, and in the other apps you go straight to the dashboard.

**Apple devices:** in some App Store review builds, the register button and the trial welcome are not shown.

## Appendix: test systems and developer builds

*For testers, app reviewers and developers.* The screens above describe the normal **release** app running against the **production** system. Three settings change what you see:

- **Release or debug build.** Store and web versions are release builds. Developers run debug builds.
- **Test system.** The app shows a red **TEST** banner in the corner. This happens in every debug build, and in a release build when the server points that app version at the test (staging) server, as is done for App Store / Play Store review versions.
- **Production or development server.** This decides whether passwords can be emailed or written to the server log.

### What changes

| Screen | Release, live system | Test system (release build) | Debug build |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start screen | Normal | Red **TEST** banner. On iPhone/iPad/Mac: **no Register button** and no banner | Red **TEST** banner |
| Registration | Empty fields | Empty fields | Prefilled with *John Doe*, *test@example.com* |
| Your password | Random, sent by email | Random, sent by email. Exception: **test…@example.com** addresses on the staging server (growerp.org) always get **qqqqqq9!** (for store reviewers) | Always **qqqqqq9!** |
| Login | Empty fields | Empty fields | Prefilled with *test@example.com* / *qqqqqq9!* (Support app: *SystemSupport* / *moqui*) |
| Forgot password | Empty email | Empty email | Prefilled with *test@example.com* |
| Company Setup: name | Empty | Empty | *Company &lt;your email name&gt;* (GrowERP system company: always *GrowERP*) |
| Company Setup: currency | You choose | You choose | US Dollar preselected |
| Company Setup: Load Demo Data | Off by default | Off by default | **On** by default |
| Welcome to GrowERP! (trial) | Shown | Shown, except on **iPhone/iPad/Mac**, where it is skipped (Apple does not allow trial messages outside in-app purchase) | Shown, except on iPhone/iPad/Mac |
| Subscription / payment | Empty card fields | Test card prefilled (4242 4242 4242 4242, 11/33, 123, *Test Customer*) | Test card prefilled, text *"Test system"* instead of *"Trial period"* |

### Emails and passwords on the server
- **Email server configured** (and not the placeholder password): the password email is sent. **Addresses containing `example.com` never receive email**, on any system.
- **No email server**:
  - On a **development** server the password is written to the server log.
  - On a **production** server it is never shown, so a self-hosted install without email must configure one first.
- **Automated test (CI) servers** (`GROWERP_TEST_MODE=true`) also let the test suite read temporary reset passwords directly. Never enable this on a reachable server.
- **Trial length** is 14 days, unless the server sets `evaluationDays`.
- **Time travel for tests:** on development and test servers, the test suite can log in "N days in the future" to test trial expiry (Scenario D). This is ignored on production.
