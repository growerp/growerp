# GrowERP — First 10 Customers: Outreach Messages

Ready-to-adapt messages for the target groups in [GrowERP_First_10_Customers_Plan.md](./GrowERP_First_10_Customers_Plan.md) §3.
Tone follows [GrowERP_Content_Voice_Guide.md](./GrowERP_Content_Voice_Guide.md): the founder writing as "I", plain and modest, no hype.

**Rules:** personalize every `[...]` part, send one by one (never as a mass mailing), and ask for a conversation, not a sale.

---

## The messages

### 1. Past client — warm (email / WhatsApp)
> Hi [Name],
>
> It's been a while since we worked on [project]. I hope [Company] is doing well.
>
> For the last few years I have been building GrowERP, an open-source system for small businesses: orders, stock, invoices and accounting in one place, on phone, tablet and desktop.
>
> It works now, and I'm looking for 10 companies to use it for real and tell me honestly what's missing. In return I set it up for free, move your data over from your spreadsheets or current system, and it costs nothing for six months.
>
> Would a 20-minute call to see if it fits [Company] make sense? If not, no problem at all. I'd still like to hear how things are going.
>
> Hans

### 2. Contact who is probably not a fit — ask for a referral
> Hi [Name],
>
> A quick question. I'm looking for small business owners (5–100 people) who still run on spreadsheets, or on a set of tools that don't talk to each other.
>
> I built an ERP system called GrowERP and I'm offering 10 of them a free setup and six months free use, in exchange for honest feedback.
>
> Do you know one or two people like that? An introduction or just a name would help me a lot.
>
> Thanks, Hans

### 3. LinkedIn connection request (max ~300 characters)
> Hi [Name], I saw [specific: your post about X / that Company is growing / we both know Y]. I build GrowERP, an open-source ERP for small businesses, and I'm learning how owners like you run day-to-day operations. Happy to connect.

### 4. LinkedIn DM — 1st-degree connection who runs an SMB
> Hi [Name],
>
> [Specific line: congratulations on the new location / I read your post about hiring / I see Company now ships to X.]
>
> I'm working on GrowERP, an open-source system that puts orders, stock, invoices and accounting together. Before I improve it further, I want to understand how businesses like [Company] actually work.
>
> Could I ask you 3 questions in a 20-minute call? Which tools you use, what takes the most time, and what you'd change. No sales pitch. If it turns out GrowERP could help, I'll offer a free setup, but that's up to you.

### 5. Follow-up — no reply after 5–7 days (send once, then stop)
> Hi [Name], just bringing this to the top in case it got buried. If the timing is wrong or it's not relevant, a short "no thanks" is completely fine. Hans

### 6. Someone commented on a LinkedIn post
> Thanks for your comment on [post topic], [Name]. You mentioned [their point]. How do you handle that at [Company] today? I'm collecting real examples, and I'd like to hear yours.

### 7. Accountant / bookkeeper — partner
> Hi [Name],
>
> You probably see more small-business administration than anyone: Excel exports, shoeboxes of receipts, three systems that don't match.
>
> I built GrowERP, an open-source ERP where orders, stock, invoices and the books are in one system, and the accountant can log in directly. I'm looking for one or two accountants to try it with a client or two. I set it up for free, and I'd like your view on what is missing from an accounting point of view.
>
> Would you be open to a 30-minute look? If it's useful for your clients, we can talk about a referral arrangement later.
>
> Hans

### 8. Reply to "sounds interesting" — book the call
> Great, thank you. Here is my calendar: [link]. Pick any 20 minutes that suit you.
>
> To use the time well: what do you use today to manage orders, stock and invoices? A one-line answer is enough.

### 9. After the discovery call — recap + next step
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for your time today. What I heard:
> - Today you use [tools].
> - The biggest time-sink is [pain], about [hours/week or problem].
> - What would help most: [their words].
>
> Proposal: on [date] I set up GrowERP for [Company] and import your [products/customers] from the file you send me. It takes about an hour together. Then you use it for one week alongside your current way of working. After that we decide together whether to continue.
>
> Does [date/time] work?
>
> Hans

### 10. Happy customer — testimonial + referral (around week 4)
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks again for using GrowERP these past weeks and for all your feedback. [Feature] is better because of you.
>
> Two small requests:
> 1. Would you write 2–3 sentences on what changed for [Company]? I would like to put them on the website with your name, only if you agree.
> 2. Is there one other business owner you think would benefit? An introduction by email is enough.
>
> No problem if not.
>
> Hans

---

## Usage notes
- Personalize every bracketed part. A generic first line gets ignored.
- Send 20 per week, one by one (plan §4). Log each one as a Lead activity in the GrowERP CRM.
- Follow up only once. Silence after that means no; move on.
- Change the wording if conversations stay below 2 a week. Ask a friendly contact why they wouldn't answer.
- Sign with your own name; swap "Hans" if someone else sends them.
