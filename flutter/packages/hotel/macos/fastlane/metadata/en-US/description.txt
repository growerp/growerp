Keep every room, booking and guest folio in one place. GrowERP Hotel takes a property from enquiry to paid invoice: see availability at a glance, check guests in and out in seconds, and let housekeeping and the front desk work from the same live plan.

Key Features:
• Room plan with drag-and-drop reservations
• Fast check-in and check-out with guest history
• Housekeeping status per room, updated live
• Room types, rates and seasonal pricing
• Guest folios, invoices, payments and receipts
• Extras and services charged straight to the room
• Revenue, occupancy and accounting reports
• Your hotel website and content, managed in-app
• Multiple properties and currencies in one account

Built for independent hotels, guest houses, hostels and B&Bs that want a real property management system without a per-room subscription. Staff can work from a tablet at the desk, a phone on the floor, or a browser at home. GrowERP is open source and runs on the Moqui and Apache OFBiz business platforms — no licence fees, no lock-in.

Made for the Mac, and synchronized in real time with the iPhone, iPad, Linux, Windows, Android and web versions.
