Put AI to work in your business without losing control of it. GrowERP Agents lets you run AI agents against your own company data, schedule them, and approve what they do before it happens — with every action recorded.

Key Features:
• Chat with agents that can see your business data
• Scheduled agent jobs that run on their own
• Approval step before an agent changes anything
• Full audit trail of every action an agent took
• Knowledge base and wiki as the agents' source material
• Tools and integrations, switched on per agent
• Company, employee and website administration
• Multiple companies, each with their own agents

For owners and managers who want the productivity of AI assistants and the accountability of a real system of record: nothing happens off the books, and you can always see who — or what — did it. GrowERP is open source and runs on the Moqui and Apache OFBiz business platforms — no licence fees, no lock-in.

Made for the Mac, and synchronized in real time with the iPhone, iPad, Windows, Android and web versions.
