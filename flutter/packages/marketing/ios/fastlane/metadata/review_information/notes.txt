Turn attention into orders. GrowERP Marketing keeps campaigns, outreach, your website and your leads in one place, so the message you publish and the follow-up you send come from the same customer record.

Sign in with the demo account supplied in the review information fields. The app connects to the public GrowERP demo backend automatically; no server configuration is required. After signing in, dismiss the onboarding dialog to reach the main dashboard — the menu then gives access to every area described in the listing. All data in the demo company is test data and may be changed freely.

GrowERP is open source; the full source of this app is at https://github.com/growerp/growerp
