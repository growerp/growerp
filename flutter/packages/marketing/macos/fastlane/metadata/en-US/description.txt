Turn attention into orders. GrowERP Marketing keeps campaigns, outreach, your website and your leads in one place, so the message you publish and the follow-up you send come from the same customer record.

Key Features:
• Campaigns with the content and the results in one view
• Social posts scheduled and published from the app
• Lead and opportunity tracking, from first touch to order
• Outreach lists and follow-up activities per contact
• Website pages, blog and images, edited in-app
• Knowledge base and wiki for your team
• Product catalogue and orders, ready to sell
• AI agents that draft and schedule routine content

For small teams and one-person marketing departments tired of paying for four tools that do not talk to each other. Draft on a laptop, approve from a phone, publish everywhere. GrowERP is open source and runs on the Moqui and Apache OFBiz business platforms — no licence fees, no lock-in.

Made for the Mac, and synchronized in real time with the iPhone, iPad, Linux, Windows, Android and web versions.
