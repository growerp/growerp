Every sale counted, every shift closed. GrowERP Shop turns a tablet, phone or computer into a till that is connected to your stock and your books: tap or scan a product, take the payment, print the receipt.

Key Features:
• Product tiles with search, categories and barcode scanning
• Cash, card and mixed payments with change
• Receipts on 80mm paper
• Tax per rate, included in the price or on top
• Refunds with stock back on the shelf
• Shifts with float, cash count and Z report
• Stock goes down with every sale
• Invoices and ledger booked automatically

Made for shops of any size. GrowERP is open source: https://github.com/growerp/growerp
