From the first order to the last receipt. GrowERP Restaurant shows every table on your floor plan, sends orders to the kitchen screen the moment they are taken, and splits the bill the way your guests want it.

Key Features:
• Floor plan with live table status
• Tabs with modifiers, extras and notes
• Kitchen display that updates by itself
• Split the bill equally or by item
• Cash and card payments with receipts
• Shifts with float, cash count and Z report
• Tax per rate, included in the price or on top
• Invoices and ledger booked automatically

Made for restaurants, cafes and bars. GrowERP is open source: https://github.com/growerp/growerp
