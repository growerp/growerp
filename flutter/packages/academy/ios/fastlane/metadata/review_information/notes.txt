Learn at your own pace with GrowERP Academy. Browse the courses your school or company publishes, enrol, and work through each module with an AI tutor at your side.

Sign in with the demo account supplied in the review information fields. The app connects to the public GrowERP demo backend automatically; no server configuration is required. After signing in, dismiss the onboarding dialog to reach My Courses — the menu then gives access to the course catalog and every area described in the listing. All data in the demo account is test data and may be changed freely.

GrowERP is open source; the full source of this app is at https://github.com/growerp/growerp
