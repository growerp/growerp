Learn at your own pace with GrowERP Academy. Browse the courses your school or company publishes, enrol, and work through each module with an AI tutor at your side.

Key Features:
• Course catalog of every published course
• My Courses: pick up exactly where you left off
• AI tutor that answers questions about the lesson
• Exercises and quizzes with instant feedback
• Review sessions to keep what you learned
• Lessons translated into your own language
• Modules that unlock as you progress
• Cohorts and discussions with fellow learners
• Learning history and progress tracking

Built for schools, training providers and companies that train their own staff. Course authors create and publish courses in GrowERP; learners follow them here on phone, tablet or desktop. GrowERP is open source and runs on the Moqui and Apache OFBiz business platforms — no licence fees, no lock-in.

Made for the Mac, and synchronized in real time with the iPhone, iPad, other main platforms and web versions.
