Get paid for every hour you work. GrowERP Freelance turns tracked time into sent invoices without spreadsheets: log time against a client or project, apply the right rate, and generate the invoice in a couple of taps.

Key Features:
• Time entries per client, project and task
• Rate cards, so each client bills at the right price
• One-tap invoices generated from approved time
• Cash in / cash out book for everything else
• Profit and loss, ready for your accountant
• Clients, contacts and follow-up activities
• Quotations and orders when a job grows
• Your own website and portfolio, managed in-app

Made for independent professionals — consultants, developers, designers, coaches and anyone billing by the hour or by the day. Log time on your phone between jobs, then invoice from your desktop in the evening; it is the same data either way. GrowERP is open source and runs on the Moqui and Apache OFBiz business platforms — no licence fees, no lock-in.

Accessible on Linux, Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and web browsers. All synchronized in real-time.
