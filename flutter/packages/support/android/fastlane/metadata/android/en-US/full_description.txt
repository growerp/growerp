Operate the GrowERP platform itself. GrowERP Support is the control app for the people running a GrowERP installation: see the companies on the system, the apps they use, and whether the backend behind them is healthy.

Key Features:
• Registered companies and their subscriptions
• App and instance administration per company
• Live REST statistics for the backend services
• Infrastructure health and resource monitoring
• Support chat with the companies on the system
• Activities and follow-up for support requests

Intended for GrowERP system administrators and hosting partners rather than end users. GrowERP is open source and runs on the Moqui and Apache OFBiz business platforms — no licence fees, no lock-in.

Available on Android and as a Linux snap.
