Run an insurance agency with GrowERP Insurance: keep every client policy in one place, renew policies before they lapse, follow claims with the carrier and track the commissions the carriers pay.

Sign in with the demo account supplied in the review information fields. The app connects to the public GrowERP demo backend automatically; no server configuration is required. After signing in, dismiss the onboarding dialog to reach the dashboard; the menu gives access to policies, renewals, claims, commissions, clients, carriers and every area described in the listing. All data in the demo account is test data and may be changed freely.

GrowERP is open source; the full source of this app is at https://github.com/growerp/growerp
