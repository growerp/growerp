Know where every item is, who has it and when it comes back. GrowERP Rental runs a hire business end to end: check availability on a visual planner, book the item out, and bill the rental for as long as it stays out.

Key Features:
• Planner showing every asset and every booking
• Instant availability check before you promise a date
• Rate cards by hour, day, week or month
• Asset register with condition and maintenance history
• Recurring billing for long-running rentals
• Reservations, contracts, deposits and returns
• Invoices, payments and full accounting
• Customers, quotations and follow-up activities
• Multiple locations and currencies in one account

Built for equipment hire, tool and machine rental, vehicle fleets, event and party hire, and anyone renting out property. Counter staff can book from a tablet while the workshop updates maintenance from a phone. GrowERP is open source and runs on the Moqui and Apache OFBiz business platforms — no licence fees, no lock-in.

Accessible on Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and web browsers. All synchronized in real-time.
